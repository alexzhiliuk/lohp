For the questions that keep coming back

Beneath what keeps returning is a pattern waiting to be seen.

Career

Is this what I really want?

Career is rarely just about work. Behind most professional questions is a deeper one. About whether the life you're building is actually the one you want.

Direction

Right? Left? Straight?

Direction doesn't always arrive as certainty. Sometimes it comes as a feeling that keeps returning. Or simply pushed aside for when you were ready.

Love

Why does the same pattern follow me?

Love leaves traces in the questions that repeat, in the patterns you almost recognise. You can't predict what's coming, but you can understand what's already moving.

Timing

Feeling it or fearing it?

Some moments ask you to wait. Others ask you to move. Learning to tell them apart is its own kind of clarity.

Loss

What remains when everything changes?

Loss leaves a silence, but if you go below the surface, it is rarely empty. You can't fill what's missing, or learn to see what's still there.
